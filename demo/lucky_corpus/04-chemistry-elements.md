# Basic Chemical Elements

Hydrogen is the first element on the periodic table, with atomic
number 1. Oxygen has atomic number 8. Carbon has atomic number 6, and
its ability to form long chains is the basis of organic chemistry.
Gold has the chemical symbol Au, from the Latin "aurum," and iron has
the symbol Fe, from "ferrum." Helium, atomic number 2, is the second
lightest element and does not react with other elements under normal
conditions.
