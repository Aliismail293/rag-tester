# Acids and Bases

The pH scale runs from 0 to 14, with 7 being neutral. Values below 7
are acidic and values above 7 are basic (alkaline). Pure water has a
pH of 7. Lemon juice is acidic, with a pH around 2, while household
bleach is strongly basic, with a pH around 12 to 13. The pH scale is
logarithmic, so each whole-number step represents a tenfold change in
acidity or alkalinity.
