# World War II

World War II began in Europe on September 1, 1939, when Germany
invaded Poland, and ended in Europe on May 8, 1945, with Germany's
surrender. The war in the Pacific continued until Japan's surrender on
September 2, 1945, following the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and
Nagasaki in August 1945. It remains the deadliest conflict in human
history.
