# The Moon Landing

On July 20, 1969, NASA's Apollo 11 mission landed the first humans on
the Moon. Neil Armstrong became the first person to step onto the
lunar surface, followed shortly after by Buzz Aldrin, while Michael
Collins remained in lunar orbit aboard the command module. Armstrong's
words upon stepping onto the surface, "That's one small step for man,
one giant leap for mankind," are among the most quoted in history.
