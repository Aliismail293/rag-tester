# The French Revolution

The French Revolution began in 1789, marked by the storming of the
Bastille prison in Paris on July 14 of that year — a date now
celebrated annually as Bastille Day, France's national holiday. The
revolution led to the abolition of the French monarchy and, in 1793,
the execution of King Louis XVI. It fundamentally reshaped French
society and influenced revolutionary movements across the world.
