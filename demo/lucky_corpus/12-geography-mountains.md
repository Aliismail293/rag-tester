# Mount Everest

Mount Everest, located in the Himalayas on the border between Nepal
and Tibet, is Earth's tallest mountain above sea level, with a peak
elevation of 8,849 meters (29,032 feet). It was first summited on May
29, 1953, by Sir Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay. While Everest is
the tallest mountain above sea level, Mauna Kea in Hawaii is taller
when measured from its base on the ocean floor.
