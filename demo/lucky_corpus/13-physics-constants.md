# Basic Physics Constants

The speed of light in a vacuum is approximately 299,792,458 meters per
second, often rounded to 3.0 x 10^8 m/s. Standard Earth gravity is
approximately 9.8 meters per second squared. Absolute zero, the
coldest theoretically possible temperature, is 0 Kelvin, equal to
-273.15 degrees Celsius. These values are treated as fixed constants
in physics and do not vary by location or experiment under normal
conditions.
