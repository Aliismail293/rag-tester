# Thistlewick Robotics — Company Overview

Thistlewick Robotics was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Port
Callendra. The company currently employs 143 people across two offices.
Thistlewick Robotics is led by its founder and CEO, Mira Oyelaran, who
previously ran the company's research division before taking over as
chief executive in 2022. The company's stated mission is "to make
precision actuation boring, reliable, and cheap." Thistlewick Robotics
is privately held and has not taken outside investment since its
founding round.
