# The Emberloop Actuator

The Emberloop Actuator is Thistlewick Robotics' flagship product. The
current model, the Emberloop EL-7, weighs 2.4 kilograms and delivers a
maximum continuous torque of 38 newton-meters. On a full charge, the
EL-7's internal battery lasts 11 hours under typical duty cycles. The
EL-7 replaced the earlier EL-6 model in March 2023 and added a sealed
housing rated for outdoor use. Every Emberloop unit ships with a
5-year mechanical warranty, which is two years longer than the
warranty offered on the EL-6.
