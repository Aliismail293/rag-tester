# The Kindlepath Program

New engineering hires at Thistlewick Robotics go through the Kindlepath
Program, an onboarding track that runs for 21 business days. Each
participant is paired with a mentor and has 4 scheduled check-ins over
the course of the program: one in the first week, two in the second
week, and a final review in the third week. Participants who complete
all four check-ins and finish their onboarding project receive a
Compass Badge, which is displayed on their internal profile. The
Kindlepath Program was introduced in 2021 and replaced an earlier,
unstructured onboarding process that had no fixed length.
