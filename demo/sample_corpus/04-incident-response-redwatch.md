# The Redwatch Protocol

Thistlewick Robotics handles production incidents under the Redwatch
Protocol, which defines three severity tiers: Ember, Blaze, and
Inferno, in increasing order of severity. An Inferno-tier incident, the
most severe tier, must receive a first response from the on-call
engineer within 12 minutes of being declared. The team responsible for
running the Redwatch Protocol is the Cinder Response Unit, a rotating
group of eight engineers. Every Inferno-tier incident requires a
written postmortem within 5 business days of resolution, reviewed by
the Cinder Response Unit lead before it is closed.
