# Customer Support Tiers

Thistlewick Robotics offers three customer support tiers: Driftline,
Anchorline, and Coreline. Driftline is the entry tier and guarantees a
response within 48 hours during business days. Anchorline guarantees
an 8-hour response during business hours. Coreline is the top tier and
guarantees a 2-hour response at any time, day or night, every day of
the year. Only customers with an active Coreline contract are assigned
a named support engineer; Driftline and Anchorline customers are
routed to the general support queue instead.
