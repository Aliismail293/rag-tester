# The Emberloop EL-6 (Legacy)

Before the EL-7, Thistlewick Robotics sold the Emberloop EL-6, which
shipped from 2020 until it was discontinued in March 2023 when the EL-7
launched. The EL-6 weighed 2.9 kilograms, noticeably heavier than the
EL-7's 2.4 kilograms, and had no sealed housing, so it was not rated
for outdoor use the way the EL-7 is. Thistlewick Robotics still
supports EL-6 units already in the field with spare parts through
2027, but all new firmware features are built for the EL-7 only.
Customers upgrading from an EL-6 to an EL-7 receive a 15% trade-in
credit.
