# Field Calibration for the EL-7

Every Emberloop EL-7 unit must be field-calibrated within 30 days of
installation. The EL-7 calibration procedure supports a maximum of 14
concurrent calibration channels per rig, and a full calibration pass
takes approximately 40 minutes per channel. Field technicians use a
handheld tool called the Calibration Wand to run the procedure; the
Calibration Wand itself must be recalibrated against a reference EL-7
unit every 90 days. Skipping a scheduled calibration voids the EL-7's
torque-accuracy warranty, though the general 5-year mechanical
warranty is unaffected.
