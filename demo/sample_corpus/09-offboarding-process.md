# The Emberdown Process

When an employee leaves Thistlewick Robotics, their departure is
handled under the Emberdown Process. IT access is revoked within 4
hours of the employee's last day being confirmed, and the departing
employee's manager must complete a handover document within 3 business
days. Emberdown does not apply to contractors, who are offboarded
immediately by their engagement manager instead. The Emberdown Process
was introduced in 2022 after an internal audit found offboarding times
varied wildly across teams; before that, there was no company-wide
standard at all.
