# Security Incident Escalation

Suspected security incidents at Thistlewick Robotics are escalated
through a chain separate from the Redwatch Protocol used for
operational incidents. The first point of contact is the on-call
security lead, who must acknowledge a report within 15 minutes. If the
security lead does not acknowledge within that window, the report
automatically pages the CTO directly. All confirmed security incidents
are logged in a register that only two people can edit: the CTO and
the head of security. The register has never been reset since it was
created in 2021.
