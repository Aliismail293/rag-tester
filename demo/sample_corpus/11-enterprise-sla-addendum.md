# Enterprise SLA Addendum

Customers on the Coreline support tier can sign an optional Enterprise
SLA Addendum on top of the standard 2-hour response guarantee. The
Addendum adds an uptime guarantee of 99.9% measured monthly, and pays
an automatic service credit equal to 5% of the monthly contract value
for every hour the guarantee is breached, up to a cap of 50% in a
single month. The Enterprise SLA Addendum is only available to
customers with at least 3 EL-7 units under contract, and it must be
renewed annually rather than automatically.
