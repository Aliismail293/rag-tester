# Expense Policy

Thistlewick Robotics reimburses employee expenses submitted within 60
days of purchase; anything older is denied automatically by the
expense system, with no manual override. Meals during business travel
are capped at 45 dollars per day. Any single expense over 500 dollars
requires manager approval before submission, not just after. The
expense system, internally called Ledgerfox, was adopted in 2022 and
replaced a manual spreadsheet process that had been in place since the
company's founding.
