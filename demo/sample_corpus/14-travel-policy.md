# Travel Policy

Business travel booked through Thistlewick Robotics' travel policy
must go through the internal booking tool, Farroute, rather than being
booked directly and expensed afterward. Flights under 6 hours must be
economy class; flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.
Employees must book at least 10 business days in advance except for
customer emergencies, which are exempt from the advance-booking rule
entirely. Rental cars require a second approval from the employee's
manager beyond the standard Farroute booking approval.
