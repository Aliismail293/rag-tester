# Data Retention Policy

Thistlewick Robotics retains customer telemetry data from Emberloop
units for 18 months before automatic deletion, unless a customer has
opted into the Extended Retention add-on, which keeps data for 5
years. Internal chat logs are retained for 90 days by default.
Security incident records, by contrast, are kept indefinitely and are
never subject to automatic deletion. Requests to delete a customer's
data early must be approved by the data protection officer, a role
that has been held by the same person since the policy was written in
2022.
