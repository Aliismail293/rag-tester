# Sales Territories

Thistlewick Robotics divides its sales organization into four
territories: North Bramble, South Bramble, Eastport, and Callendra
Metro, the last of which covers the area around company headquarters.
Callendra Metro is the only territory with a dedicated field sales
team; the other three are served remotely. Each territory has its own
quarterly quota, and Eastport has consistently had the smallest quota
of the four since territories were first defined in 2021. A single
account can only belong to one territory at a time, determined by the
billing address on file.
