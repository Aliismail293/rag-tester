# Partner Certification Program

External integrators can become certified to install and service
Emberloop units through the Ironclasp Certification Program. Ironclasp
certification requires passing a written exam and a hands-on
installation review, and it must be renewed every 2 years. There are
two levels: Ironclasp Associate, which covers installation only, and
Ironclasp Master, which also covers field calibration and warranty
repairs. As of the program's last review, fewer than 40 partners
worldwide hold Ironclasp Master certification.
