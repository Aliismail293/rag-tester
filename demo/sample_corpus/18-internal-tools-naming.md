# Internal Tools Naming Convention

Thistlewick Robotics names most of its internal tools after fire or
ember imagery, a tradition that started with the Redwatch Protocol and
has stuck ever since: the expense system is Ledgerfox, the travel
booking tool is Farroute, and the offboarding process is the Emberdown
Process. New internal tools are supposed to follow this convention, but
it is enforced only loosely; the engineering team's internal wiki is
simply called "the Wiki" and nobody has ever proposed renaming it. Any
new tool name must be approved by the internal tools committee before
it ships company-wide.
