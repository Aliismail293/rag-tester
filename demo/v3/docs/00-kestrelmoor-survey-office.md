# Kestrelmoor Survey Office

The Kestrelmoor Survey Office is an invented coastal agency that measures tides, charts shoals and publishes sea data for harbour pilots. It was chartered in 1987 and is headquartered in the town of Fenwick Strand.

It runs five tide stations, four survey vessels, four data products and three regional offices. Field work follows numbered procedures in the KS series.

Every station, vessel and office reports to the duty hydrographer, who signs off each day's readings before they are published.
