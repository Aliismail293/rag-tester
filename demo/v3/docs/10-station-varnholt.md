# Varnholt tide station

Varnholt is the oldest of the Kestrelmoor tide stations, built on a basalt spit in 1991.

A crew of four lives on site. Supplies arrive by boat every 9 days, weather permitting.

The main gauge is recalibrated every 45 days against a portable reference float.

If sustained wind passes 70 km/h, the crew moves to the inland hut and the gauge records unattended. The radio call sign is KS-VAR.
