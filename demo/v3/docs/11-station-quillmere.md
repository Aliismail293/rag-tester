# Quillmere tide station

Quillmere sits at the mouth of a tidal lagoon and was opened in 2003.

Two technicians staff it in rotating fortnights. A supply boat calls every 14 days.

Its gauge drifts quickly in the lagoon silt, so it is checked against the reference float every 21 days.

Storm rules here are stricter: once wind holds above 55 km/h, the technicians shelter in the concrete store. On the radio it answers to KS-QUI.
