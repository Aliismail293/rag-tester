# Ashgrove Point tide station

Ashgrove Point was the second station to be built, in 1996, on a cliff above deep water.

Six people work there, the largest crew in the network. Deliveries come up the cliff railway every 7 days.

Because the water is deep and calm, its gauge only needs recalibrating every 90 days.

Staff stay at their posts until gusts reach 85 km/h; above that they retreat to the bunker below the lighthouse. Call sign: KS-ASH.
