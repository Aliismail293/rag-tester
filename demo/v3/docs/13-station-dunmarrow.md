# Dunmarrow tide station

Dunmarrow is the newest station, commissioned in 2016 on an artificial island.

It is crewed by three engineers. A helicopter brings provisions every 12 days.

Salt spray fouls the sensors, so the gauge is recalibrated every 30 days.

When the wind stays above 60 km/h the engineers lock the instrument room and wait in the accommodation module. The station's radio name is KS-DUN.
