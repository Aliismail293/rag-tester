# Pellicot Reach tide station

Pellicot Reach guards a narrow estuary channel and has operated since 2008.

Just one keeper lives there, with a relief keeper every second month. Stores are dropped every 20 days.

The estuary gauge is compared with the reference float every 60 days.

The keeper leaves the gauge house for the stone cottage whenever wind exceeds 65 km/h. Its call sign is KS-PEL.
