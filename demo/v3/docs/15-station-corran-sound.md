# Corran Sound tide station

Corran Sound watches a fast tidal race between two islands. It was the fourth station in the network, completed in 2001.

Three observers share the work, each on a six-week tour. Fresh food and fuel come over on the mail boat every 10 days.

Currents in the race scour the gauge mounting, so the instrument is checked against the reference float every 28 days.

At a steady 75 km/h the observers stop work and move into the old coastguard hut on the hill. Radio operators know it as KS-COR.

The station also logs water temperature every hour, which the Swell Index uses to correct its wave model.
