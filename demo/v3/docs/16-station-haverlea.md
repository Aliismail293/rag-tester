# Haverlea tide station

Haverlea stands at the head of a long, shallow bay and has recorded tides since 2012.

Its staff is two wardens, who also run the bay's bird count. A van brings supplies along the causeway every 6 days at low water.

Mud in the bay smothers the sensor head, so the gauge is recalibrated every 14 days, more often than at any other station.

The wardens shelter in the visitor centre as soon as the wind passes 50 km/h. The call sign is KS-HAV.

Because the causeway floods, nobody may cross it within 2 hours of high water.
