# RV Selkie Moss

RV Selkie Moss is a shallow-draught launch built in 2011 for estuary work; her home port is Quillmere.

At 19 metres she is the smallest ship in the fleet, with a crew of 5.

Her echo sounder is rated to 120 metres, enough for every channel the Office charts.

Her yearly maintenance falls in November, when estuary traffic is lowest.
