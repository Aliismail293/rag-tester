# RV Marlow Quay

RV Marlow Quay is a former fishing trawler that the Office bought and converted in 2014. She works out of Haverlea.

Her length is 27 metres and she sails with 8 aboard.

The single-beam sounder fitted during the conversion works down to 350 metres.

She is slipped for her annual refit every April, before the spring survey season.

Unlike the rest of the fleet she can tow a sediment sled, which the Littoral Digest relies on.
