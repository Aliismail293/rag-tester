# Procedure KS-31: chart correction

KS-31 is used when a survey finds the Shoal Atlas is wrong.

It has 8 steps and a correction must be issued within 72 hours of the survey.

Signatures: the survey scientist, the product editor and the chief hydrographer.

If the error is shallower than charted by more than 1 metre, pilots are warned by radio before the paperwork starts.
