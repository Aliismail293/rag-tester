# Data buoy Gannet

Gannet is a moored wave buoy anchored in 85 metres of water off Varnholt. It went into service in 2010.

Its batteries are topped up by two solar panels and last about 9 days without sun.

A crew visits it every 120 days under procedure KS-25.

It sends a wave report every 30 minutes and a full spectrum every 3 hours.
