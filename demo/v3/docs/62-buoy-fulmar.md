# Data buoy Fulmar

Fulmar marks the entrance to the Quillmere lagoon, in just 12 metres. It is the oldest buoy, from 2006.

A small wind turbine charges it, and the reserve covers 5 days of calm.

Weed growth is heavy at the lagoon mouth, so it is serviced every 60 days.

It reports every 10 minutes and sends a spectrum every hour.
