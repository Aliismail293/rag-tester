How it works

HOW IT WORKS · 1 / 6

Why two checks?

A right answer can come from the model's memory. Checking the page separately catches that.

Why can't the score be faked?

Questions avoid the page's exact wording, a second model checks every answer key, and the final exam stays locked during tuning.

Why the same questions every run?

Scores only compare fairly on the same quiz. ragaudit keeps your questions while your documents stay the same, and writes new ones only for pages you add or edit.
EASIEST WAY · 2 COMMANDS

Install once, then set everything up in your browser.

Open a terminal (Terminal on Mac, PowerShell on Windows), paste each line, press Enter.

1Install ragaudit (once)
$ pip install ragaudit-cli
2Open the setup page in your browser, then click Run
$ ragaudit ui
PREFER THE TERMINAL? EVERY COMMAND
ragaudit doctorChecks Python, your AI settings and your adapter, and says how to fix anything missing
ragaudit init --framework demoWrites a ready-to-run adapter; use plain, langchain or llamaindex for your own app
ragaudit check --docs ./docs --adapter ragaudit_adapter.pyOne quick test (about 20 questions), then opens this report
ragaudit improve --docs ./docs --adapter ragaudit_adapter.pyThe self-improving loop: tunes settings on practice, scores on the locked exam
ragaudit report --openRe-opens the latest report

What you need

Python 3.11 or newer, a folder of documents, and either Ollama (free, runs on your computer) or an API key. The setup page checks all three for you.

Where results go

Every run is saved in a .ragaudit folder next to your documents. That is what the score history reads.

Your data stays local

With Ollama, nothing leaves your computer. Reports are files on your disk, not web links.
